UCLA, the Unversity of California, Los Angeles, is a public research university in Los Angeles and part of the University of California system.

UCLA at a glance
Type: Public research university.
Location: Los Angeles, California.
Academics: UCLA offers undergraduate, graduate and professional education across a broad range of disciplines.

UCLA offers graduate education across a broad mix of academic, research, and professional disciplines. Its official site says there are master's and doctoral programs in 130+ academic and professional fields, with more than 40 graduate programs ranked in the national top 10.
